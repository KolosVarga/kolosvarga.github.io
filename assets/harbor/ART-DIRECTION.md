# The sheltered cove

`painted-cove.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project.
The pier, boats, rigging, people, cat, birds, pennants, reflections and ripples are separately
drawn and animated in `js/harbor.js`. The background contains only the coast, buildings, sky
and open water. This separation keeps nudges small and preserves the painting.

The user supplied the Ponyo trailer as an atmosphere reference. The video could not be
loaded in this environment; the artwork follows the user's written characteristics.
No film frames, characters or third-party artwork were copied into the project.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create one wide landscape painted background for an interactive harbor illustration on a personal portfolio. Original Ghibli-inspired warm coastal animation background: lush gouache and watercolor on textured paper, delicately imperfect hand-drawn ink, quiet sunny late afternoon, sage and pine green wooded headlands, terracotta roofs and small harbor buildings at far left, distant blue islands, billowy pale clouds, muted clear jade seawater. Canvas wide landscape about 3:2. Camera a slightly elevated view looking across sheltered harbor. Important compositing constraint: the entire lower 55 percent of picture is ONLY open turquoise water with soft painterly horizontal brush marks, no ships, no boats, no piers, no characters, no ropes, no text: those will be rendered as separately animated layers. Horizon about 35 percent from top, a wooded coastal hill frames left side occupying upper left 40 percent, tiny offwhite lighthouse on distant right headland; warm soft golden natural sunlight and rich subtly mottled painted pigments. Charming environmental detail in distant town: laundry lines, potted plants, weathered boathouse. Strong craft, beautiful traditionally painted animated feature film scenery, peaceful and inviting. No photorealism, no 3D rendering, no gradients as graphic design, no lettering, no logos, no borders.
